A note from our founder

Built by a shop owner, for shop owners

I spent 15 years at American Express working with small businesses, and I saw how much great local shops bring to their towns. Now I own and run Mega Brown Box, a discount and overstock store in La Habra, California. It's Townshelf's first store and the model for how every shop lists, grades and ships.

My mission is simple: help local shops succeed and compete with big-box retailers by putting their quality goods in front of shoppers everywhere, under their own names.

Mitch Rolam, Founder · Read more
