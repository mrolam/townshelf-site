Listing standard

Every listing, the same honest format

Consistent listings help shoppers trust every shop. Full guide: LISTING-TEMPLATE.md

Clear title

Brand + item + key detail + size/color. For example: “[Brand] Stand Mixer, Tilt-Head, 5-Qt, Red.”

Grade + what's included

New, Open Box or Like New, plus exactly what's in the box and anything that's missing.

3–6 real photos

Front, back, label/tag, any flaws, and one for scale. Photos of the actual item.

Price & retail

Your price, plus the original retail price if you know it.

Size & weight

Packed dimensions and weight, so shipping is quoted correctly.

Short, honest description

2 to 3 sentences on condition, testing and anything a buyer should know.
